# Questwheel v9.1

v9.1 includes all v9 features plus retrospective manual jar entries on the day/week wheels.

## Manual jar entries on the wheel
When you use + Time on a jar, the required activity name, date, time, and minutes now also create a visual history segment on the wheel.

Example:
- What: Took a shower
- Time: 10:15
- Minutes: 10

The jar still receives +10m exactly as before, and the wheel shows a 10:15–10:25 completed segment in that jar's colour.

Manual wheel segments:
- are slightly translucent with a dashed edge, to distinguish them from planned blocks;
- are already-completed historical records;
- do NOT affect the Free Time bank;
- do NOT double-credit the jar;
- can carry across midnight if necessary;
- also appear on weekly mini-wheels.

The wheel's centre/weekly hour totals remain scheduled-time totals, so retrospective manual entries do not inflate the amount you originally scheduled.
